# Decision record

Compare Jailer direct SQL subset export with Tonic Structural masking/subsetting and its potential destination database requirement. Selection criteria: local deployability, infrastructure footprint, extraction completeness, defect fidelity, masking, review effort, total cost and operational turnaround. No purchase decision until a synthetic PoC succeeds. Product/trial claims require current vendor verification.
